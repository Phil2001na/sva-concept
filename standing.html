What the regulators do

Why a register matters.

Public Accountants and Auditors Board

The statutory body that regulates the public accountancy and auditing profession in Namibia. Only registered firms may carry out audits.

Institute of Chartered Accountants of Namibia

The professional institute for chartered accountants in Namibia, which sets membership standards and lists its members' practices.

Descriptions of the two bodies are general background and should be confirmed against their own websites.
