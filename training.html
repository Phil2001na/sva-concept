Training office

Where trainee accountants earn their experience.

As an accredited training office, the firm trains aspiring chartered accountants in public practice, alongside the audit and tax work it does for clients.

Life as a trainee

Real work, with real supervision.

Public practice is where a trainee sees many businesses, many ledgers and many problems in a short time. Trainees work inside the same audit and tax teams that serve the firm's clients.

Audit exposure

Work on engagements alongside audit seniors, supervisors and managers who plan and review the work.

Tax exposure

Learn how tax questions arise for businesses and individuals, and how they are resolved.

Guidance

Experience is gained under qualified accountants who review the work and explain their reasoning.

A recognised route

Accreditation as a training office means the experience gained here counts toward professional qualification.

The details of the training programme, intake and requirements are set by the firm and the professional bodies. Please contact the office to ask.
