Services

Audit and tax, explained plainly.

Two disciplines under one roof, each run by a dedicated team. Here is what each involves and how the work usually unfolds.

Audit and assurance

An independent opinion on your financial statements.

Audit engagements are planned and directed by a team of audit seniors, supervisors and managers. The aim is a clear, supportable opinion on whether your financial statements give a true and fair view.

  1. Planning

    We learn your business, its risks and its reporting needs, and agree the scope and timetable of the engagement with you.

  2. Fieldwork

    The team tests records, controls and balances, and discusses findings with your finance staff as the work proceeds.

  3. Review

    Supervisors and managers review the work before any conclusion is reached, so that the opinion rests on sufficient evidence.

  4. Reporting

    You receive the audit report together with observations on matters that came to our attention, and a chance to discuss them.

Taxation

Tax advice for businesses and individuals.

Tax consulting across the areas of taxation that businesses and individuals deal with. The examples below show the kind of questions the tax team handles; ask the office about your own situation.

Income tax

Returns, assessments and planning for companies, partnerships and individuals.

Value-added tax

Registration, returns and queries on how VAT applies to your transactions.

Employees' tax

Payroll tax obligations for employers, and the records that support them.

Dealing with the revenue authority

Help preparing correspondence, objections and responses to queries.

Services are described in general terms for this design concept. The firm confirms the exact scope of each engagement directly with the client.
